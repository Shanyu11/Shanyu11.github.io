Staged Rockets

Solid-propellant rockets with UCSD Rocket Propulsion Laboratory. The team is building a two-stage demonstrator as the prototype for a 15 ft Spaceport America Cup vehicle.

Timeline: Oct 2025 to present Tools: OpenRocket · MATLAB · SolidWorks

Problem and result

Multi-stage rockets fail most often at staging. Before committing to a 15 ft rocket candy vehicle (35% sorbitol / 65% potassium nitrate) for the Spaceport America Cup, the team built a scaled demonstrator to prove the staging mechanism in flight. The mechanism is our own design, reviewed with SpaceX engineers.

Result Clean stage separation in flight. Both stages and the avionics were recovered.

Mechanism details and flight footage stay private at the team's request until the competition vehicle flies.

G-class vehicle

900 ft/s

A 24.21 in long, 1.6 in diameter G-class rocket, simulated in OpenRocket and MATLAB and flown at the Friends of Amateur Rocketry site. It reached 900 ft/s and was recovered with its avionics intact.
