Robot Interpretation
of Human Speech

Co-authored research with a group at the University of Oxford. Do household robots still do the right thing when the same command is phrased differently?

Timeline: Oct 2025 to present Tools: Python · PARTNR Planner simulator

Question

People phrase instructions however they like. We tested whether a household robot executes the same instruction correctly when it is framed in different ways.

Method A robot simulator (PARTNR Planner) ran 4,670 household-chore instructions across 17 framing types: 1 neutral baseline and 16 variants. I did the data validation, the statistical analysis of task-success rates, iteration on the framings, and the figures.

Findings

The paper is under review at TACL, so the results are held until publication.
