Robot Interpretation
of Human Speech

Co-authored research from the University of Oxford: do household robots still do the right thing when the same command is phrased differently?

Role: Data validation, statistical analysis, figures Timeline: Oct 2025 – present Status: Submitted to TACL Tools: Python · PARTNR Planner simulator

Question

Voice-controlled robots have to act on instructions people phrase however they like. We investigated whether household robots correctly execute human instructions when the same command is framed in different ways — a core reliability question for voice-controlled robotics.

Method Robot simulator (PARTNR Planner) executing framed instructions on household chores: 4,670 instructions across 17 framing types — 1 neutral baseline + 16 variants. My role: data validation, statistical analysis of task-success rates, iteration on framings, and the paper's figures.

Findings

Detailed results are held for publication while the paper is under review at TACL. The study measured task-success rates across 4,670 instructions spanning 17 framing types — 1 neutral baseline plus 16 variants — executed in the PARTNR Planner household-chore environment.
