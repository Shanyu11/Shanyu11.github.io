Robot Interpretation
of Human Speech

Research with a group at the University of Oxford. The question: if you give a household robot the same instruction in different words, does it still do the right thing?

Timeline: Oct 2025 to present Tools: Python · PARTNR Planner (a household robot simulator from Meta)

Objective

People word instructions however they like, so a voice-controlled robot has to handle many phrasings of the same request. We tested this in PARTNR Planner, a simulator where a robot and a virtual human do household chores together. The robot was given 4,670 instructions written in 17 different framings: one plain baseline and 16 variations in wording. For each one we recorded whether the robot completed the task. My part was validating the data, running the statistical analysis of task-success rates, refining the framings, and making the figures. The paper is submitted to TACL (Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics), a peer-reviewed MIT Press journal, and the results are held until it is published.
