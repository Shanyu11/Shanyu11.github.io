BEED

A benchtop instrument that does a multi-machine sample prep workflow in one device. I built it myself, from user interviews to a bench-tested prototype. Design details are confidential until the patent is filed.

Timeline: Jun 2026 to present Tools: ESP32 · C++ · SolidWorks · 3D printing

Demo

Rev 3

The problem

Sample prep in a formulation lab runs across several machines. Scientists carry samples from one to the next, re-secure them, and watch each step, all day.

During my internship at Bayer / Vividion Therapeutics I interviewed a formulation scientist and lab engineers about this. I turned what they told me into requirements for workflow fit, cost, and buildability, then built the machine.

Goal One device that runs the full prep cycle in place. Based on the interviews, it removes the machine swapping and saves a scientist 5+ hours a week.

Technical

  • Control: ESP32 firmware in C++ that sequences the full prep cycle.
  • Electronics: custom driver circuits for the actuation subsystems.
  • Mechanical: 3D-printed enclosure modeled in SolidWorks, three revisions.
  • Trade-offs: component cost, heat, and driver power.

Firmware and design documents stay private until the patent is filed. I can walk through both in an interview.

Validation and next

Bench tests compared both core functions against the standalone equipment the device replaces. Next: duty-cycle testing and a pilot in a real formulation workflow.
