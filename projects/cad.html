Why it's here

This is pre-college work, and it's presented as exactly that: the foundation. These six mechanisms are where I learned the habits the newer projects run on — designing around fabrication constraints (CNC vs. printed parts), iterating geometry against real failure modes like jams and wheel lift, and integrating sensors into mechanical design. The through-line from a bearing-mounted turret to a staging mechanism or a bench-validated lab instrument is shorter than it looks.
