Rotating Turret

Turret on a lazy-Susan bearing that aims the scoring mechanism without moving the robot. CNC-machined plates, geared for fast and repeatable positioning.

Why it's here

This is high school work, and I show it as the foundation. These six mechanisms are where I learned to design around how a part gets made, to fix geometry against real failures like jams and wheel lift, and to put sensors inside mechanical designs.
