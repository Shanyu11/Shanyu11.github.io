CAD Mechanism
Library

Six robot mechanisms I designed in high school for FIRST Tech Challenge, a robotics competition where teams build a small robot to score game pieces in a yearly game. Three seasons with team Innov8rz, newest first. Each card opens a GitHub page with photos, a 3D model you can rotate in the browser, and the full CAD assembly.

Timeline: 2020 to 2023 Tools: Fusion 360 · CNC · 3D printing

Power Play

Rotating Turret

A turntable on a lazy-Susan bearing that aims the scoring arm without turning the whole robot. CNC-machined plates, geared for fast and repeatable aiming.
CNC · Lazy-Susan bearing
View CAD ↗

Auto-Trigger Clamp

3D-printed gripper with a color sensor that closes on the cone by itself when it sees it. The tapered opening guides the cone in, so an off-center approach still works.
3D printed · Sensor-actuated
View CAD ↗

Freight Frenzy

Dual Linear Slides Outtake

Two telescoping rails that lift freight up to the shelf. Using a pair splits the load, so the lift stays rigid through repeated cycles.
Aluminum extrusion · Linear rails
View CAD ↗

Mirrored Dual-Sensor Clamp

Symmetric gripper with a sensor on each face, so the robot can pick up from either side without turning around.
3D printed · Bilateral symmetry

Ultimate Goal

Multi-Stage Ring Intake

Collects rings off the floor, lines them up, and feeds the launcher in one path. Each stage is tuned for speed and fewer jams.
3D printed · Staged feed

Dual-Motor Flywheel Outtake

Two-motor spinning-wheel launcher for high-arc ring shots. Tuned for wheel speed, spin-up time, and shot-to-shot consistency.

Why it's here

This is high school work, shown as the foundation. These six mechanisms are where I learned to design around how a part gets made, to fix geometry against real failures like jams and wheel lift, and to build sensors into mechanical designs.
