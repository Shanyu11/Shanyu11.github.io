Combat Robot

Mechanical design for UCSD Triton Robotics in the RoboMaster competition. The robots shoot projectiles at each other, take hits, and keep driving.

Timeline: Jan to Oct 2025 Tools: SolidWorks · 3D printing · CNC

42 mm

I redesigned the 42 mm launcher to sit lower on the robot. A lower center of gravity made the robot more stable and faster.

Suspension

Mecanum

I redesigned the mecanum-wheel suspension on a new chassis for better traction and stability on uneven arena terrain.
