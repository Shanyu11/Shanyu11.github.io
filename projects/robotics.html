RoboMaster
Combat Robot

RoboMaster is an international university robotics competition. Teams build driven robots that shoot small projectiles at each other, take hits, and keep driving. I did mechanical design for Triton Robotics, UC San Diego's team.

Timeline: Jan to Oct 2025 Tools: SolidWorks · 3D printing · CNC machining

Launcher

Description

The previous robot was top-heavy and lost grip on the uneven arena floor. Two redesigns fixed that. The launcher that fires 42 mm projectiles was moved lower on the chassis, which dropped the center of gravity and made the robot faster and more stable. The suspension for the mecanum wheels (wheels with angled rollers that let the robot drive sideways) was rebuilt on a new chassis for better traction. The robot competed in the 2025 season.
