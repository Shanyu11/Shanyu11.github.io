Automated Benchtop
Lab Instrument

A benchtop instrument that automates a routine lab workflow in one device. Built from user interviews to a bench-tested prototype. What it does is confidential until the patent is filed.

Timeline: Jun 2026 to present Tools: ESP32 · C++ · SolidWorks · 3D printing

Demo

Rev 3

Problem and result

A routine lab workflow runs across several machines, with manual handling between every step. During my internship at Bayer / Vividion Therapeutics I interviewed a scientist and lab engineers about it, turned that into requirements for workflow fit, cost, and buildability, and built the instrument.

Result One device that runs the whole workflow in place. Bench tests matched the standalone equipment it replaces. Projected to save a scientist 5+ hours a week.

Technical

  • Control: ESP32 firmware in C++ that sequences the full cycle.
  • Electronics: custom driver circuits for the actuation subsystems.
  • Mechanical: 3D-printed enclosure modeled in SolidWorks, three revisions.

Firmware and design documents stay private until the patent is filed.
