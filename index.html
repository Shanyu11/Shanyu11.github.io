I'm a mechanical engineering student at UC San Diego, specializing in robotics and controls, with a second degree in human biology — which is why my projects keep landing where machines meet medicine: lab automation, clinical data tooling, and medical device work.

I like owning the whole build. On the rocket team it means designing mechanisms that either work at 900 ft/s or fail where everyone can see it.

Experience: data engineering intern at Bayer / Vividion Therapeutics (clinical trial automation), AI engineering intern at Merantis (multimodal ML for medical device compliance), and a research summer in AI/ML and NLP at the University of Oxford.
