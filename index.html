About

Shanyu Chauhan

I'm a mechanical engineering student at UC San Diego specializing in robotics and controls, with a second degree in human biology — which is why my projects keep landing where machines meet medicine: lab automation, clinical data tooling, and medical device work.

I like owning the whole build. On the lab instrument that meant doing the user interviews, defining the requirements, writing the firmware, breadboarding the electronics, modeling the enclosure, and running the bench validation myself. On the rocket team it means designing mechanisms that fly or very visibly don't.

Experience: data engineering intern at Bayer / Vividion Therapeutics (clinical trial automation, current), AI engineering intern at Merantis (multimodal ML for medical device compliance), and a research summer in AI/ML and NLP at the University of Oxford.
